Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Zvipfuwo account? +
Download the app and navigate to the Sign Up screen. Provide your name, email, and choose a strong password. You will immediately be set up with a secure farmer profile.
Is my livestock data secure? +
Yes, absolutely. Zvipfuwo uses PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) to isolate your farm database records. Your animals, treatments, and transaction histories can only be accessed by you.
Does the app require internet to run? +
Yes, Zvipfuwo requires an internet connection to securely synchronize your data with the cloud database. If connection is lost, the app will advise you of the offline status.
How can I delete my data? +
You can request account deletion by contacting support. Deleting your account will trigger a complete cascade, permanently erasing your profile and all associated livestock records from our databases.
